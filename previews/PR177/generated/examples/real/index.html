Cosine and Sine Transforms

For real data with even or odd symmetry the non-equidistant fast cosine and sine transforms (NFCT, NFST) replace the complex NFFT. NFFT.jl uses the definitions of NFFT3: for a real array $\bm{f}$ of size $\bm{N}$ and nodes $\bm{k}_j \in [0, 1/2]^D$,

\[\text{NFCT:}\quad \hat{f}_j = \sum_{\bm{n}=\bm{0}}^{\bm{N}-\bm{1}} f_{\bm{n}} \prod_{d=1}^D \cos(2\pi n_d k_{d,j}), \qquad \text{NFST:}\quad \hat{f}_j = \sum_{\bm{n}=\bm{1}}^{\bm{N}} f_{\bm{n}} \prod_{d=1}^D \sin(2\pi n_d k_{d,j}).\]

The array index of mode $n_d$ is $n_d + 1$ for the NFCT and $n_d$ for the NFST. Instead of an adjoint, the real transforms have a transpose.

using NFFT, LinearAlgebra, Random
Random.seed!(1)

k = rand(2, 1000) ./ 2            # nodes in [0, 1/2]²
f = randn(64, 48)

p = plan_nfct(k, size(f))
fHat = p * f
g = transpose(p) * fHat
size(fHat), size(g)
((1000,), (64, 48))

The plans take the same options as the NFFT (reltol, dims, eltype, nthreads, window, W, σ) and are checked here against the direct evaluation:

relerr(a, b) = norm(a - b) / norm(b)
relerr(fHat, NDCTPlan(k, size(f)) * f), accuracy(p)
(3.534271968423461e-9, 5.936924253460223e-9)
q = plan_nfst(k, size(f); reltol=1e-12)
relerr(q * f, NDSTPlan(k, size(f)) * f), accuracy(q)
(4.3758863964184735e-13, 9.79728031815724e-13)

How it works

The NFCT is an NFFT of the even extension of $\bm{f}$ (size $2\bm{N}$), the NFST one of the odd extension. Its oversampled grid of $2\tilde{\bm{N}}$ points is even (odd) about $0$ and $\tilde{\bm{N}}$, so only the points $0, \dots, \tilde{N}_d$ are stored:

  1. Resampling correction: divide by $\hat\varphi$ as in the NFFT.
  2. FFT: a DCT-I (DST-I) along each dimension, computed as a real FFT of the extended line.
  3. Resampling: the halo of the grid is filled with mirror images (with a sign change for the NFST), so the resampling loops are the ones of the NFFT, on a real grid.

The cost is about that of a complex NFFT of size $\bm{N}$:

tmin(f) = (f(); minimum(@elapsed(f()) for _ in 1:5))
kc = rand(2, 2^18) ./ 2; kf = rand(2, 2^18) .- 0.5
fr = randn(256, 256); fc = complex.(fr)
pc = plan_nfct(kc, (256, 256)); pf = plan_nfft(kf, (256, 256))
yr = zeros(2^18); yc = zeros(ComplexF64, 2^18)
(NFCT_ms = 1e3 * tmin(() -> mul!(yr, pc, fr)), NFFT_ms = 1e3 * tmin(() -> mul!(yc, pf, fc)))
(NFCT_ms = 7.597624, NFFT_ms = 7.592571)
